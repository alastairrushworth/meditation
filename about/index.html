About this collection

This is a hand-tended index of free guided meditations — the practices, not the talks. A script reads the RSS feeds of 42 insight-meditation and dharma centres each week, keeps the episodes that are actually guided sittings, and files them by teacher, length and type of practice.

Nothing here is hosted or re-published. Audio streams from the original publisher and every title links to the centre's own page, so the centres keep their traffic and their attribution. The site carries no advertising and sells nothing.

The collection covers mindfulness and the body scan, breath and concentration practice, loving-kindness, compassion and equanimity, and practices for difficult emotions — from teachers including Jill Shepherd, Tara Brach and Mark Nunberg, and from centres including Common Ground Meditation Center, Insight Meditation Community of Washington DC, Lokanta Vihara and Auckland Insight Meditation.

Licensing

Every recording is published by its centre under a Creative Commons licence that permits non-commercial sharing of the unmodified talk with attribution (CC BY-NC-ND 3.0 and CC BY-NC-ND 4.0). Each is credited to its teacher and centre with a link to its licence, and the audio streams from the publisher's own servers rather than being copied here.

The licence is read from each feed on every weekly build. A centre that stops declaring an open licence stops being used, and its recordings leave this site on the next run — which is why several well-known meditation podcasts that reserve their rights are not listed.
